# Stuck log

Write here when you get stuck — **before** you ask for a reference solution.

`./ttt reveal <stage>` will not unlock until this file has an entry for that
stage. That isn't bureaucracy: writing down what you expected and what actually
happened is the single most effective debugging technique there is, and most of
the time you'll solve the problem while writing the entry and never need the
reveal.

Copy this block and fill it in:

```
Stage:
Problem:

What I expected to happen:

What actually happened:

What I've tried:

What I think is going on:
```
